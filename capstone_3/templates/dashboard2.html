Note on data sources: Population and area figures come from the Copernicus Urban Atlas, which defines urban boundaries by Functional Urban Area (city + commuter belt) rather than administrative city limits. When these boundaries differ significantly from the municipal area, metrics like tree cover percentage and population-to-tree ratio may not perfectly reflect conditions within the official city boundary. For example, Eisenstadt (Austria) is listed with ~31K FUA population vs ~16K city proper, and its 0.03% tree cover applies within a 4 km² urban core rather than the full 42.9 km² administrative area. Always consider the source definition when interpreting individual city rankings.
