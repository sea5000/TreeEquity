Methodology

Tree cover percentage is calculated as tree_cover_km² ÷ urban_area_km² × 100 for each city. Equity categories are assigned dynamically by quartile breaks of the population-to-tree ratio (people per km² of tree cover). Lower ratios indicate better tree equity — fewer people sharing each km² of canopy. Dataset: 98 European cities.
